FAQ

    

      What do I need to send to get a quote?
      
Your region (Europe, Asia, NA West, NA East or South America), your faction, your game server, your character name, and the amount of Kinah you want.

    

    

      How is the price decided?
      
It depends on the server and the amount. Send us your order and we will give you a quote before anything is agreed.

    

    

      How does delivery work?
      
You list a material on the in-game auction house at the agreed price. We buy it and send you a screenshot of the purchase, then you check your Kinah and confirm.

    

    

      Do you need my account password?
      
No. We will never ask for your password.

    

    

      How do I pay?
      
We confirm the payment method with you once the price is agreed.

    

    

      Which servers do you cover?
      
Europe, Asia, North America West and East, and South America, for both Elyos and Asmodian. Ask us if you need something not listed.
